# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Small-business owners (clinics, studios, shops, service businesses) looking for a freelancer to build or replace their website. They are usually not technical. They arrive wanting to know what Lori can build for their business and whether it's worth reaching out.

## Product Purpose
A one-page site for Lori Battouk, a frontend developer and web designer in Toronto. It explains what she builds for small businesses and gets visitors to send a project inquiry. Success means a visitor sends the contact form.

## Positioning
A single person who designs and builds the whole site, including the parts small businesses usually need a separate vendor for: an online store, online booking, and an AI assistant that guides visitors to the right product or service.

## Capabilities and Constraints
What she offers (the core content of the site):
- Custom-designed, fast, responsive websites (design and frontend build).
- Online stores and e-commerce: product catalogues, filters, cart, Stripe checkout.
- AI assistants that guide visitors and recommend the right products or services (Gemini-powered).
- Online booking and scheduling systems, including integrations with tools like JaneApp.

Stack used: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Stripe, Supabase, Gemini API, Vercel/Railway.

The contact form opens the visitor's mail client (mailto) addressed to Lori; there is no backend.

Project case studies are deliberately NOT shown on the site (user decision, 2026-11-20). Do not add a work/portfolio gallery.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Lori Battouk. Based in Toronto. Studying Software Engineering Technology at Seneca Polytechnic. Available for freelance.
- Links: GitHub https://github.com/LoriB14, LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/in/loribattouk/
- Voice: plain, direct, confident; talks to business owners, not developers.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, client logos, pricing, or metrics exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Lead with what the business gets, not the tech.
2. Every section moves the visitor toward sending an inquiry.
3. Show capability through the craft of this site itself.
4. Never fabricate proof.
