# Surface: home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: small-business owners deciding whether to hire Lori. Action: send a project inquiry. Proof: no case studies (user decision); working demos and the site's own craft. Constraints: palette pinned by the user (mandarin, forest green, crest yellow; switched from midnight/pink on 2026-11-20); clean digital look, real photographs, rich motion (user: previous version 'felt like AI slop with no animations'), no physical textures, no neon sign (user rejected both, 2026-11-20); must feel like a $10k build; mailto contact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site proves what it sells. Every service is shown working, not described. It refuses the static freelancer page of claims and icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Forest green ground with drenched mandarin and crest-yellow fields (services stack, process, contact). Archivo at 125% width, weight 800 for display; Archivo body. Licensed small-business photographs (florist, cafe, bakery). Mandarin pill buttons with a crest fill wipe. Motion: Lenis smooth scroll, masked line reveals on headings, hero photo clip reveal, rotating verb, sticky stacking service panels, process line draw, footer wordmark rise.

STORY: The visitor learns in one line what Lori builds and for whom. The hero assistant asks about their business and recommends a setup that prefills the inquiry. They try the store, booking and assistant demos, then send the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: h1 left with rotating verb, sub, mandarin "Start a project" and a link, service chips below. A florist photograph on the right with the live project assistant overlapping its lower-left corner.

FORM: The category standard (canon) executed with the user's pinned palette, replacing the earlier Shop Window pick at the user's request. seed: none (pinned) — "do regular digital colors... go with the colors i said".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
